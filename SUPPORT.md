# Support for Sinhala Captions Canva App

Thank you for using Sinhala Captions for Canva! We are here to help you get the best experience generating synchronized Sinhala subtitles for your videos.

## Common Help & Troubleshooting

### 1. How to get a free Google Gemini API Key:
1. Visit [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey).
2. Sign in with your Google account and click **"Create API Key"**.
3. Copy the key and paste it into the **Style** tab in the Canva app or your local desktop engine.

### 2. Subtitles not synchronizing with video playback:
- Make sure to download the **.VTT** subtitle file by clicking **"Download sinhala_captions.vtt"**.
- In Canva, select your video, click the **Captions** icon in the toolbar, and select **"Upload subtitle file"**.

### 3. Connection issues:
- Ensure the local engine (`SinhalaCaptionsEngine.exe`) is running on your computer.
- Test connection in the **Style** tab under **Cloud Backend API URL** (`http://localhost:8000`).
