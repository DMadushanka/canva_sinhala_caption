# Privacy Policy for Sinhala Fonts for Canva

Last updated: October 12, 2026

## Overview
Sinhala Fonts ("the App", "we", "our") is designed to provide effortless Sinhala typing and font conversion for Canva creators. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy.

## Information We Collect and Process
1. **Client-Side Text Conversion:** All Sinhala text typing, phonetic conversion (Singlish, Wijesekara), and font encoding conversions (FM, DL, Unicode) occur **100% locally within your web browser**. 
2. **No Data Storage or External Transmission:** We do not store, log, transmit, or retain your text, designs, or personal data on external servers. 
3. **Personal Information:** We do not collect names, email addresses, payment information, or account credentials.
4. **Analytics & Tracking:** The App does not contain third-party tracking scripts, advertising trackers, or personal tracking cookies.

## Canva Platform Compliance
The App operates strictly within the Canva design platform and complies with Canva's Developer Guidelines, Security Standards, and Privacy Policies.

## Data Retention and Security
Because all text processing is performed locally in memory during your active editing session, closing the application immediately clears all session memory.
