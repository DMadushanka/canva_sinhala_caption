# Privacy Policy for Sinhala Captions Canva App

Last updated: October 11, 2026

## Overview
Sinhala Captions ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Canva application and companion local engine handle your data.

## Information We Collect and Process
1. **Audio and Media Files:** When you use the App to transcribe audio or video, media files are processed solely for generating Sinhala speech-to-text transcriptions and subtitle timestamps. Media files are processed temporarily and are not stored permanently on any external servers.
2. **API Credentials:** If you provide a Google Gemini API Key, it is stored locally on your device (in your browser storage and local configuration) to authenticate requests directly with Google AI Studio. We never collect, transmit, or store your personal API keys on our servers.
3. **Analytics and Personal Data:** We do not track personal identifying information, sell user data, or share personal information with third parties.

## Third-Party Services
The App interacts with:
- **Google Gemini API:** When generating transcriptions, audio data is processed according to the Google Cloud / Google AI Studio Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
- **Canva Platform:** The App operates within the Canva design platform and complies with Canva's Developer Guidelines and Privacy Standards.

## Data Retention and Security
- Transcribed media and subtitle files exist temporarily during your editing session.
- You have full control over your subtitle files (.vtt, .srt) and can delete or modify them at any time.
