# Privacy Policy for Sinhala Voice Typing, Fonts & AI Captions Studio

*Last updated: October 13, 2026*

This privacy policy explains how **Sinhala Voice Typing, Fonts & AI Captions Studio** ("the Extension") handles user data.

## 1. Overview
We believe your privacy is fundamental. The Extension is designed to operate locally within your browser side panel to provide Sinhala voice typing, font conversions, and subtitle tools. 

**We do NOT collect, sell, monetize, or track your personal data, typing history, or browsing activity.**

## 2. Data Handled by the Extension
- **Voice Typing:** Voice input is processed locally in your browser using the Chrome Web Speech API. Audio is not recorded or stored on our servers.
- **Font Conversion:** Text converted between Sinhala Unicode and font encodings (such as FM and DL fonts) is processed entirely offline on your device using client-side JavaScript.
- **Storage:** Settings, font preferences, custom uploaded fonts, and optional Gemini API keys are saved locally in your browser's private `chrome.storage.local`.
- **Canva Text Insertion:** The Extension only interacts with your Canva canvas when you explicitly click "Send to Canva". No document data is extracted or transmitted.
- **Google Gemini AI (Optional):** If you use the optional AI features (grammar polish or video captioning), requests are sent directly from your browser to Google Generative AI endpoints using your own Google Gemini API key. No third-party intermediary servers are used.

## 3. Data Sharing and Third Parties
We do not share, sell, or transmit any user data or credentials to third parties or advertising networks.
